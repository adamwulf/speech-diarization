    Models, licenses and credits

    Speech-to-text with Whisper tiny.en, base.en, or small.en via Transformers.js. Speaker diarization with diarization-js, a port of pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1 by the pyannote.audio team: pyannote segmentation-3.0 (MIT), WeSpeaker ResNet34 speaker embeddings (pyannote/wespeaker-voxceleb-resnet34-LM), and community-1 PLDA parameters; the embedding and PLDA models are licensed CC BY 4.0 (ONNX artifacts). Models run locally with ONNX Runtime Web; only model files are downloaded, never your audio.
